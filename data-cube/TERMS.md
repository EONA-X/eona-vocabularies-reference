# W3C RDF Data Cube Vocabulary (`qb`)

Upstream: [The RDF Data Cube Vocabulary](https://www.w3.org/TR/vocab-data-cube/), W3C Recommendation, 16 January 2014. Namespace `http://purl.org/linked-data/cube#`.

`v2014/ontology.ttl` is the Turtle served at `http://purl.org/linked-data/cube` (content negotiation, `Accept: text/turtle`), unmodified. It declares no `owl:imports`, so no deviation from the offline-build rule applies. Its own `owl:versionInfo` ("0.2") is the upstream working-draft number, not the Recommendation date used for `dcat:version`.

Used by `eonax-chart-profile`, which builds on `qb:DataSet`, `qb:Observation` and `qb:dataSet`.
