# Eona-X ODRL Profile for Travel Data

Extension of the [Eona-X ODRL Profile](../eonax-odrl-profile/v0.0.8/ontology.ttl) v0.0.8 for policies on personal travel data (eona-x/backlog#1319). Namespace `https://eona-x.eu/vocabulary/odrl-profile-travel#`, prefix `eox-policy-travel`; release `v0.1.0` is identified by `owl:versionIRI <https://eona-x.eu/vocabulary/odrl-profile-travel/v0.1.0#>`.

Value sets are those of [DPV 2.3](../dpv/v2.3/ontology.ttl) and [DPV-PD 2.3](../dpv-pd/v2.3/ontology.ttl). The shapes that check them are in `eonax-odrl-profile-shapes/v0.0.9`; their value lists are generated by `tools/dpv-shape-values/generate.py`.

## Status

Every term is `eox-policy:AwaitingConnectorSupport`: no connector stores or evaluates them, and EDC's policy validation rejects a policy definition that uses one (HTTP 400) until the connector declares them (eona-x/backlog#1322). A policy that uses them:

- names both profiles in `odrl:profile` (this release IRI and `https://eona-x.eu/vocabulary/odrl-profile/v0.0.8#`);
- keeps a permission the connector enforces, such as `odrl:use` constrained by `eox-policy:Membership eq "active"` (`eox-policy:KeepAnEnforcedPermission`, a rule of the base profile that `eox-policy-travel:travelRewriteRules` includes as a member): a permission whose only constraints are travel terms evaluates as empty.

## Left operands

| Term | Operators | Right operand | Rule kinds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `odrl:purpose` | `odrl:eq`, `odrl:isPartOf`, `odrl:isNoneOf` | full IRI of a descendant of `dpv:Purpose` (132) | permission, prohibition (also in `odrl:or`); not duty, obligation, remedy, consequence |
| `eox-policy-travel:personalDataCategory` | `odrl:eq`, `odrl:isPartOf`, `odrl:isNoneOf` | full IRI of a descendant of `dpv:PersonalData` (242: 11 in `dpv:`, 231 in `pd:`) | permission, prohibition, `odrl:delete` duty or obligation |
| `eox-policy-travel:consentStatus` | `odrl:eq` | full IRI of a descendant of `dpv:ConsentStatus`: valid for processing on a permission (2), invalid for processing on an `odrl:delete` duty or obligation (8) | permission; direct constraint of an `odrl:delete` duty or obligation; not in `odrl:or` |
| `eox-policy-travel:dataField` | `odrl:eq`, `odrl:isPartOf` | http(s) IRI or `urn:` of a property of the asset's data model (open set, form checked only) | every rule kind |

`odrl:purpose` is an ODRL 2.2 term reused as-is and annotated in this profile, as `odrl:elapsedTime` is in the base profile.

## Right operands

A right operand is a plain string holding the full IRI, or a JSON array of such strings for `odrl:isPartOf` and `odrl:isNoneOf`:

```json
{ "odrl:leftOperand": "odrl:purpose", "odrl:operator": "odrl:eq", "odrl:rightOperand": "https://w3id.org/dpv#ServiceProvision" }
```

The compact form (`dpv:ServiceProvision`), the `http://` form of the DPV namespace, a value outside the set and `{ "@id": ... }` are refused by the shapes. `{ "@id": ... }` is refused by upstream EDC since eclipse-edc/Connector#6164 (a right operand is a literal).

## Withdrawal of consent

There is no withdraw action. Withdrawal is an `odrl:delete` duty of a permission (or a policy-level obligation) constrained by `eox-policy-travel:consentStatus eq "https://w3id.org/dpv#ConsentWithdrawn"` and, usually, `odrl:elapsedTime lteq "PT24H"`. `odrl:delete` is Declared in the base profile (dataspace-ecosystem MR !150, open). It is never the action of the permission itself: the connector drops such a permission whole, with its constraints (eona-x/backlog#1065). The example is `eox-policy-travel:WithdrawalIsADeleteDuty` in the ontology.
